Raw output of studies/measure_query_orthogonality.py and
studies/measure_attention_collapse.py. Kept verbatim so the numbers quoted in
MIRFLICKR_DIAGNOSTIC_PLAN.md sections 4.2 / 4.2.1 stay traceable.

Caveat on the two MIRFLICKR blocks below: they were produced BEFORE the script
was corrected, so their "Inter-query redundancy" figures are the *uncentered*
cosine, which is ~1.0 for any pair of near-uniform distributions and therefore
uninformative (two diametrically opposite preferences also score 0.999). They
also lack the score_stats / three-level sections. The VOC block at the bottom
is from the corrected script and is the one to cite. Re-running the two
MIRFLICKR checkpoints through the current script is still pending.

================================================================================
Root cause (see MIRFLICKR_DIAGNOSTIC_PLAN.md 4.2.1)
================================================================================

query_tokens never left initialization scale:
  norm at init (trunc_normal_ std=0.02, dim 384) = 0.3919
  measured after 40 epochs                       = 0.3775 - 0.4047
  growth ratio                                   = 1.009

because compute_ortho_loss() normalizes Q (scale-invariant, zero pressure on
the norm), AdamW weight_decay=0.0005 shrinks it, and lr=1e-5.

Score spread 0.0952 -> softmax predicted LL=0.268 / others=0.244, measured
LL=0.265 / 0.247 / 0.242 / 0.246. The model sits exactly where the arithmetic
puts it.

Required for real selectivity: spread 0.69 / 1.50 / 2.48 for a dominant band at
40% / 60% / 80%, i.e. ||q|| of ~2.8 / ~6.2 / ~10.2 instead of 0.39.

Why the original VOC figure showed total LL dominance: it applied
scores = raw_scores * 10.0 while nn.MultiheadAttention scales by
1/sqrt(head_dim) = 1/sqrt(48) -- a 69x logit inflation. Feeding the measured
shares through that same factor reproduces the published-style figure
(LL=0.875, LH=0.100, HL=0.019, HH=0.006, entropy 0.66 bits).
