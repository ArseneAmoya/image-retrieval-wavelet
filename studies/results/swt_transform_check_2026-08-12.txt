Raw output of studies/verify_swt_transform.py, run to check that the SWT
pipeline really emits 4 distinct sub-bands in the layout the networks expect.
Triggered by mflickr_single_band_ablation returning ~82% for every band.

Verdict: the transform is correct. The tensor contract holds and the four
sub-bands are genuinely distinct (mean off-diagonal cosine 0.038). Nothing to
fix in the decomposition.

Open point (NOT resolved by this check): there is no Normalize step in the SWT
configs, and the detail bands arrive far below the input scale DINOv2 was
pretrained on -- HH has std 0.0242 against ~1 for an ImageNet-normalized input,
i.e. ~27x smaller. Whether that matters is a separate question about the
patch_embed operating range (Conv2d with bias: if ||W*x|| << ||b|| every patch
token collapses toward b), and has not been measured. Note this is NOT an
argument for ImageNet normalization specifically -- sub-bands are not natural
images -- but for checking whether the signal reaches the backbone at all.
